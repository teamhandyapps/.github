# Team Handyapps

We build websites and apps for practice, planning, cricket stats and play.

## Websites

- **[Ultimate Aptitude Prep](https://ultimateaptitudeprep.com/)** - Practice aptitude, verbal reasoning and puzzles with timed questions and explanations. Use it for placement or exam preparation.
- **[The Calculator World](https://thecalculatorworld.com/)** - Explore loan, savings and investment scenarios with online finance calculators. Compare inputs and projections before making a plan.
- **[IPL Records](https://iplrecords.com/)** - Browse IPL batting and bowling records, player comparisons and team head-to-head stats. Look up a record or compare players side by side.
- **[BBL Records](https://bblrecords.com/)** - Browse Big Bash League batting and bowling stats, season winners and team matchups. Look up a season record or explore a matchup.
- **[AQI Tracker](https://aqitracker.com/)** - Browse air-quality readings for Indian cities and monitoring stations. Find a city or view the nearest station with location permission.

## Apps

- **[3-2-5 Card Game (Teen Do Paanch)](https://325cardgame.com/)** - An Android card game with offline play against bots, online rooms with friends and an in-app rules guide. Visit the website for details.
